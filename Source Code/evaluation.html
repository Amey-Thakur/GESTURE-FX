Palm-flip detector: measured error

A rigid hand is rotated about its own long axis by a known angle at a known rate, projected orthographically and perturbed by landmark noise. The instant the rotation passes ninety degrees is therefore known exactly, and the detector's report is compared against it.

Nothing here is precomputed
